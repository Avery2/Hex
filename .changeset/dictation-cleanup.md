Clean up what you said instead of pasting the raw transcript.

Fillers fenced off with commas ("so, like, we could"), accidental repeats of function words,
punctuation and paragraph breaks are handled by default, and the expanded transcript shows exactly
what was removed — one tap puts any of it back. A "Full" level also collapses spoken
self-corrections to the version you landed on. Transforms → Clean up dictation, which shows each rule as a before/after example.

Rules only, no model: measured over 528 real transcripts, every deletion was a filler or a doubled
function word (2.2% of words), and nothing is ever added, reordered or rewritten.
